05. Model Architecture & Training

Popularity-Based Filtering

Recommends top-trending and globally popular products when user interaction data is unavailable.

  • Loved by most customers globally
  • Solves cold-start problems for new users
  • Based on purchase frequency metrics
  • Fast and lightweight computation

Hybrid Recommendation System

Delivers smarter recommendations by blending collaborative and content-based filtering models.

  • Combines collaborative & content features
  • Maximizes recommendation accuracy
  • Reduces data sparsity limitations
  • Highly diverse personalized suggestions

User-Based Collaborative Filtering

Identifies users with similar purchasing history to suggest items bought by peer groups.

  • Clusters users with similar tastes
  • Leverages historical purchase patterns
  • Personalized without needing item metadata
  • Adapts dynamically as user behavior shifts

Item-Based Collaborative Filtering

Discovers products that are frequently bought together across transactions.

  • Finds closely related catalog items
  • High stability for large user bases
  • Ideal for "Frequently Bought Together"
  • Scalable item matrix computation

Content-Based Filtering

Matches product metadata, titles, and descriptions using feature extraction.

  • Compares rich product characteristics
  • Independent of other users' actions
  • Effective for niche product discovery
  • Transparent & explainable recommendations

07. Assessment Insights

Popularity-Based Model Assessment

Achieved the highest baseline accuracy (Precision 8.23%, Hit Rate 32.23%, NDCG 8.71%). However, it suffered from extremely low coverage (0.13%) and 0% personalization, delivering identical recommendations to all users.

Hybrid Model (Optimal Solution)

Proved to be the best overall model, combining high precision (5.41%), a strong Hit Rate (18.13%), and top-tier Personalization (98.34%) across all metrics.

User-Based Collaborative Filtering

Delivered exceptional personalization (99.31%) and dynamic catalog coverage (30.74%). Accuracy metrics were limited by dataset sparsity.

Item-Based Collaborative Filtering

Outperformed User CF in precision and stability while yielding higher overall reliability across large user sessions.

Content-Based Filtering

Demonstrated solid individual performance (Precision 4.79%, Hit Rate 17.86%) with high personalization (97.98%) by leveraging product metadata.
